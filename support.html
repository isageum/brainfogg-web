Before you write

  • An event didn't get created. Open the Log tab and find the email you forwarded. The line under it says what happened — no dates found, too large, already added. If it looks wrong, press and hold that line and tap "Something look wrong?" so we can look at what we misread.
  • Nothing arrived at all. Check that you forwarded to your own Brainfogg address (Settings › Email Forwarding). Forwarding as an attachment doesn't work — send it as a regular forward.
  • Your partner can't see an event. Sharing has to be on for both of you. Settings › Sharing.
  • Billing. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel from Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. It is a real deletion, not a hidden archive. More about deletion.
