Privacy Policy

Brainfogg · Effective July 24, 2026

Brainfogg helps parents turn school emails into a shared family calendar. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep the app’s data footprint small on purpose — less data is a feature, not an afterthought.

The short version: We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use any advertising or analytics tracking. You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Brainfogg (“we,” “us”) is the provider of the Brainfogg mobile app. You can reach us at hello@brainfogg.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, your name, and (optionally) a phone number you provide for notifications.
  • Children you add: the child’s first name, grade, gender, school name, activities, and any short note you choose to enter. You decide what to add.
  • Your schedule: events, checklists, and related details you create or that are generated from emails you forward.
  • Forwarded emails: when you forward a school email to your Brainfogg address, we receive that email’s content so we can turn it into calendar events (see “AI processing”).
  • Device token: a push-notification token so we can send reminders and alerts.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active subscription. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see or store your card or bank details.

We do not collect location, contacts, browsing activity, advertising identifiers, or health/medical records through the app.

How we use your information

We use it only to provide and operate the app: to build your family calendar, extract events from emails you forward, send you reminders, share a schedule with your co-parent, and manage your subscription. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI processing of forwarded emails

To turn a forwarded email into calendar events, the email’s content is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude AI), which extracts details like dates, times, and titles and returns them to us. This happens only for emails you choose to forward. Anthropic processes this content to provide the parsing result; it is not used to show you ads.

Children’s data

Brainfogg is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children. Any information about a child is entered by you, the parent, and is used only to organize your family’s schedule. You control it: you can edit or remove a child, and deleting your account removes this information as described below. We do not knowingly let children create accounts or use the app directly.

Who we share data with (service providers)

We share data only with the providers that make the app run, and only as needed to operate it:

  • Supabase — secure database and backend hosting.
  • Anthropic — AI parsing of emails you forward.
  • Apple & RevenueCat — processing and managing subscriptions.
  • Expo — delivering push notifications.
  • SendGrid — receiving the emails you forward to your Brainfogg address.
  • Google and Apple — if you choose to sign in with them, to verify your identity.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of our users. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Sign in with Apple & “Hide My Email”

If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one. That’s fine — Brainfogg works the same way. Notifications sent to that address are forwarded to you by Apple.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data only while your account is active. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we permanently and immediately delete your personal data. If you are the only person in your family, your children, events, checklists, and forwarded-email records are deleted too. If a co-parent remains, your personal data is removed and the shared family calendar stays with them. Deletion is real deletion — we do not keep “just-in-case” copies.

Deleting your Brainfogg account does not cancel any subscription you bought through Apple. To stop billing, cancel the subscription in the App Store (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Security

Your data is protected in transit and at rest, and access is restricted to your own family through database-level access rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and to collect as little of it as possible.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your information in the app, and you can delete your account and its data at any time from Settings. If you have questions or a request about your data, email us at hello@brainfogg.ai.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact
