Delete Your Account & Data

Brainfogg AI (ai.brainfogg.app) — how to delete your account and all associated data.

Delete inside the app (immediate)

Open Brainfogg and go to Settings → Delete account. After a two-step confirmation, your account is deleted immediately and permanently. This is a real deletion, not a deactivation:

  • If you are the only parent in your family, everything is deleted — your children’s profiles, all events, checklists, and every record of emails you forwarded.
  • If a partner remains in the family, your account and the records of the emails you forwarded are deleted, and your name is removed from shared events. The shared family calendar stays with your partner, since it is their data too.
  • Events Brainfogg mirrored to your device calendar are removed as well.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email hello@brainfogg.ai from the address you signed up with (or include it in your message) and ask us to delete your account. We verify the request against the sign-up address and complete the same permanent deletion described above, normally within 7 days. We will confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

Account identity (name, email, phone number), children’s profiles, all events and checklists, records of forwarded emails, and push notification tokens. Deletion is real deletion — we do not keep “just-in-case” copies. For the full picture of what Brainfogg stores, see our Privacy Policy.
